Frequently asked questions

Go to paypal.com and click Having trouble logging in? Enter your email address or phone number, and we'll send you a link to reset your password. Check your spam folder if you don't see the email within a few minutes.

Standard transfers to a bank account take 1–3 business days. If you need the funds faster, you can use Instant Transfer (a fee applies) and receive your money in minutes to an eligible debit card or bank account.

PayPal may limit your account if we need more information to verify your identity, detect unusual activity, or if there's a policy violation. Log in and visit the Resolution Center to see what steps are needed to restore full access.

Go to your Activity page, find the transaction, and click on it. Scroll down and select Report a problem. Choose I didn't authorize this transaction and follow the steps. We'll investigate and get back to you within 10 days.

Log in to your account and go to Wallet. To add a bank, click Link a bank account and follow the prompts. To remove, select the bank and choose Remove bank account. Note: you cannot remove your primary funding source until another is set.

Payments that are pending (the recipient hasn't yet claimed them) can be cancelled from your Activity page. However, once a payment is completed and accepted, you'll need to request a refund directly from the seller, or open a dispute through the Resolution Center.
